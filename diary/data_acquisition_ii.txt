Since our data is being collected from APIs, we are able to programmatically alter the structure to how we see fit.
The Developer posts data is collected from the forem dev.to API and inserted into a sqlite database, while the News data is streamed from Hugging Face and put into CSVs.
Once enough News Articles have been collected, the CSVs are merged into a single file, which is then converted into a sqlite file via pandas.
Once both datasets are in their own sqlite files, we merge them into a single database file, each with their own table.
