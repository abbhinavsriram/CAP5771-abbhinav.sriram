Problem 1: Topic Drift in Developer Articles

Importance: The core validity of the dataset depended on it. The AI-tagged articles on DEV.to and Hashnode skewed toward tutorials and implementation guides rather than commentary about AI's impact on the community.

Rule applied: Drop using via clustering and zero-shot classification to filter out technical articles.

Before vs. after: 25,672 articles -> 5,398

Risks remaining: Around 2,000 retained articles were still flagged as technical by zero-shot classification, meaning some noise is still in the data. The difference between technical and non-technical articles is subjective, and some articles discussing AI ethics through a technical lens may have been dropped unfairly. Therefore, authors who might lean toward technical explanations of major issues in AI will be underrepresented by the data.
