What is the main story or message your visualizations or article communicate?
Our visualizations are meant to show how the news media and developers view and discuss AI differently. We do this by showing
differences in sentiment towards AI, showing a pie chart breaking down the sentiment distribution of the news articles and dev posts.
We also are interested in how the news media and developers view and discuss AI differently, which is shown through various
graphs, one being the coverage of subtopics on AI over time. There are a few obvious take aways that the user shuold be able to see, like:
- News coverage of AI is about 10% points more positive than posts from the developer community
- News coverage of AI in terms of ethics has declined over the years, while the legal side of AI has grown in terms of coverage
- In both Developer and News coverage, sentiment towards AI is decreaesing over time
