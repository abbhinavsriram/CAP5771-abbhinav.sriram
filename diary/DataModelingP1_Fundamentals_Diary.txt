Here's a refined version:

Model Choice & Reasoning: We chose Facebook's BART model for zero-shot classification. Since our data was unlabeled with respect to our desired categories, a supervised model would not be ideal. BART allowed us to get probabilistic category assignments without any fine-tuning, leveraging its large pretraining corpus with reasonable accuracy.

Problem Type: This was a classification task. Zero-shot classification is well-suited here since we define the label space ourselves without needing labeled training data.

Interpretability/Complexity: The model itself is not interpretable, but the probabilistic outputs provide some confidence since low-confidence scores flag articles that may be miscategorized. While BART is a complex model, the pipeline was relatively simple since no training was required on our end.

Data Size: ~5,400 developer articles and ~10,000 news articles, each averaging ~1,500 words. Only the first 1,500 characters were used per article due to token limits.

Model Assumptions: The model assumes the topic category can be reliably inferred from the first 1,500 characters, which ignores any introductory prelude that may not be representative of the article's core topic.
