In this project, we aim to explore the differing perspectives on AI between developers and mainstream media. Specficially, we plan to center our analysis on the following issues
- Context/prompt window issues
- Environmental impact
- Hallucinations
- Layoffs / automation
- Licensing / IP infringement

To do this, we plan to create a few distinct datasets to guide our analysis
1. Developer perspective dataset: 
- WhWe plan to scrape data from a popular tech forum, dev.to, which contains ~300,000 articles since 2017 written by developers on a variety of topics, ranging from technical to more conceptual/ethical discussions.
- dev.to has a functional API through Forem, which is how we plan to retrieve our data. We will be filtering articles from late 2019 to present day.
- Since it's a developer forum, most of the articles are bound to be more focused on technical implementations rather than conceptual discussions. Combined with a lack of proper filtering through the forem API (no method to filter by multiple tags at once), we might need to use keyword searching in addition to tag-based filtering to ensure that we don't ingest unnecessary articles that might seem relevant, but are rather just technical implementations rather than real developer perspectives
