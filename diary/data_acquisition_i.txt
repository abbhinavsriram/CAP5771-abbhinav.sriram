In this project, we aim to explore the differing perspectives on AI between developers and mainstream media. Specficially, we plan to center our analysis on the following issues
- Context/prompt window issues
- Environmental impact
- Hallucinations
- Layoffs / automation
- Licensing / IP infringement

To do this, we plan to create a few distinct datasets to guide our analysis
1. Developer Perspective Dataset:
- We plan to scrape data from a popular tech forum, dev.to, which contains ~300,000 articles since 2017 written by
    developers on a variety of topics, ranging from technical to more conceptual/ethical discussions.

- dev.to has a functional API through Forem, which is how we plan to retrieve our data. We will be filtering articles
    from late 2019 to present day.

- Since it's a developer forum, most of the articles are bound to be more focused on technical implementations rather than
    conceptual discussions. Combined with a lack of proper filtering through the forem API (no method to filter by multiple tags
    at once), we might need to use keyword searching in addition to tag-based filtering to ensure that we don't ingest
    unnecessary articles that might seem relevant, but are rather just technical implementations rather than real developer perspectives

2. Mainstream Media Dataset
- We plan on collecting news articles from traditional mainstream news sources by utilizing the Infini News Corpus dataset.
    This is a dataset that contains news articles from a bunch of different publishers between 2021 to 2025.

- The Infini News Corpus dataset is distributed through Hugging Face, so by streaming the data from Hugging Face,
    we are able to continuously download articles and in real time filter out any article that doesn't seem A.I. related.
    We determine if an article is A.I. related by checking for occurrences of a few keywords.

- The biggest limitation with this dataset is that it contains a lot of data, most of which is not relevant to our project.
    So, while we do have basic filters, it is possible that we have included articles that are not focused on A.I.,
    but may mention it in relation to a different topic.
