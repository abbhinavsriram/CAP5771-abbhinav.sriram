# CAP5771-abbhinav.sriram
1. What are you trying to do? Articulate your objectives using absolutely no jargon.
In this project, we are trying to understand how developers and mainstream media view AI automation concerns. We believe that the two perspectives are distinct and unique since developers will tend to approach the topic more critically given that they are more exposed to how AI models function on a lower level compared to mainstream media (who are more likely to see it as a black box).

2. How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?
There is literature out there which compares different media sources coverage of AI. However, there is currently no major studies/analytics that gauges developer sentiment vs. mainstream media coverage of AI automation.
